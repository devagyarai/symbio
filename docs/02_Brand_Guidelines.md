# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity
**Symbio** is a professional, modern, and reliable SaaS platform. The brand voice is clear, authoritative, yet approachable, focusing on productivity and security.

## Visual Language
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, minimal, and modern. We prioritize content and usability over unnecessary embellishments.
- **Theme:** Full support for both Light and Dark modes. Dark mode is treated as a first-class citizen for developer and power-user comfort.
- **UI Components:** Utilizing `shadcn/ui` for a sleek, accessible, and consistent design language across the application.

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** Inter (or similar modern sans-serif) for high legibility across diverse screen sizes.
- **Hierarchy:** Clear distinction between headings, subheadings, and body copy to facilitate scanning.

## Color Palette (Conceptual)
- **Primary:** A strong, trustworthy color (e.g., modern indigo or deep blue) for primary actions and branding.
- **Backgrounds:** Clean white/off-white for light mode, deep slate/gray for dark mode.
- **Accents:** Semantic colors for states (success green, warning yellow, danger red).

## Tone of Voice
- **In-App Messaging:** Concise, helpful, and encouraging.
- **Documentation:** Professional, technical, and structured.
- **Marketing:** Visionary, focusing on value propositions like security, speed, and collaboration.
