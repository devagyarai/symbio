# Product Vision

## Vision Statement
**Symbio** is the collaborative workspace for modern organizations.

## Core Philosophy
We believe that modern teams require more than just a task tracker; they need an integrated, secure, and highly responsive environment where work happens in real time.

## Key Tenets
1. **Uncompromised Security & Isolation**
   Every organization deserves complete peace of mind. Our strict multi-tenant architecture ensures that tenant data is rigorously isolated.
2. **Real-Time Collaboration**
   Work doesn't wait. Whether it's task updates, comments, or file sharing, everything in Symbio happens in real time, eliminating the need for context switching.
3. **Premium User Experience**
   Enterprise software does not have to be clunky. Symbio delivers a consumer-grade, highly responsive, and accessible interface (complete with Dark Mode) that users will love.
4. **Comprehensive Oversight**
   Trust but verify. With detailed analytics and comprehensive audit logging, organizations maintain full visibility and compliance over their workspaces.

## Future Outlook
Symbio will continually evolve to meet the needs of modern organizations, expanding its ecosystem of integrations while maintaining its core focus on speed, security, and simplicity.
