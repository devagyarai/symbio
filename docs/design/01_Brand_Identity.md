# Brand Identity: Symbio

## 1. Complete Brand Identity
Symbio is designed for modern organizations. We do not just build software; we build a calm, highly performant environment where work happens effortlessly. 

**Core Adjectives:**
- **Premium:** Every interaction feels crafted. High signal-to-noise ratio.
- **Modern:** Unbound by legacy UI constraints. We embrace the latest in web capabilities (glassmorphism where it serves a purpose, sub-pixel rendering).
- **Minimal:** Invisible UI. The interface should disappear, leaving only the user's content and goals.
- **Calm:** Work is stressful enough. The tool shouldn't add to it. Low contrast where appropriate, muted tones, and silent transitions.
- **Intelligent:** Anticipates user intent. Keyboard-first navigation. Contextual menus over static toolbars.
- **Fast:** Instant feedback. Optimistic UI updates.
- **Collaborative:** Multi-player by default, but respectful of deep work.

**The Logo & Logomark:**
The Symbio mark should be geometric, sharp, and timeless. No gradients in the primary mark. A single, solid color (typically our primary stark white or deep black, depending on the theme) that scales flawlessly from a 16px favicon to a billboard.

## 8. Illustration Style
We reject the overly colorful, whimsical "corporate memphis" illustration style. 

**Principles:**
- **Abstract & Geometric:** Use subtle, 3D geometric shapes (spheres, cubes, glass planes) rendered with soft lighting.
- **Monochromatic/Duotone:** Illustrations should primarily use our neutral palette, with a single accent color used sparingly to draw the eye.
- **Technical & Precise:** Graphics should look like they belong in an architectural blueprint or a high-end hardware manual, not a children's book.
- **Purposeful:** Illustrations are only used when they explain a concept better than text (e.g., in empty states or feature onboarding).

## 16. Landing Page Visual Direction
The Symbio landing page is the first physical touchpoint of our brand. It must set a tone of immense quality and speed.

**Visual Anatomy:**
- **Hero Section:** Massive, crisp typography (using our Display font). A singular, powerful tagline: *The Collaborative Workspace for Modern Organizations.*
- **The Product as the Hero:** High-fidelity, edge-to-edge product screenshots or seamless looping videos of the product in action. The UI is our best marketing asset.
- **Lighting:** "Dark mode by default" for marketing. It creates a sense of depth, premium quality, and cinematic focus.
- **Subtle Motion:** Scroll-triggered reveals should feel buttery smooth, driven by spring physics, not linear delays.
- **Micro-interactions:** Buttons that have a subtle magnetic pull; borders that illuminate on hover (a la Vercel/Linear).
- **Social Proof:** Logos of modern, respected companies presented in a subdued, monochromatic treatment.
